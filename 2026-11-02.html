Marvell 收购 Lumentum

Wall St Engine (@wallstengine)

Marvell（$MRVL）将以 1150 亿美元全股票交易收购 Lumentum（$LITE）
Marvell 已同意以全股票交易方式收购 Lumentum，企业价值约 1150 亿美元，将两家 AI 基础设施与光网络技术的主要供应商合二为一。
根据协议：
• 每股 LITE 可换得 4.5 股 Marvell 股票，相当于每股约 1132 美元。
• 收购价较 10 月 28 日未受消息影响的收盘价溢价 23.9%。
• Marvell 股东将持有合并后公司约 67% 的股份，Lumentum 股东持有 33%。
这笔交易把 Marvell 的定制 AI 芯片、网络交换机和互连技术，与 Lumentum 的激光器、光模块和光路交换能力结合在一起。
更大的机会在于从铜互连向光互连的加速转变：AI 数据中心需要更快的数据传输、更高的带宽和更好的能效。
Marvell 表示，合并后其可触达市场有望扩大到 5500 亿美元，能够在 AI 数据中心内部以及先进芯片封装中提供更一体化的互连方案。
两家公司还预计：
• 每年超过 10 亿美元的成本协同效应。
• 长期营收增速和利润率均将提升。
• 交易完成后第一年即可增厚 Marvell 的调整后每股收益。
Matt Murphy 将继续担任合并后公司的 CEO，交易预计于 2028 年上半年完成，尚需股东和监管机构批准。
另外，Marvell 重申了本财年第三季度营收指引的中值，并将指引区间从 ±5% 收窄至 ±2%。

原文：$MRVL TO ACQUIRE $LITE IN $115B ALL-STOCK DEAL
Marvell has agreed to acquire Lumentum in an all-stock transaction valued at approximately $115B in enterprise value, combining two major suppliers of AI infrastructure and optical networking technology.
Under the agreement:
• Lumentum shareholders will receive 4.5 Marvell shares for each LITE share, implying roughly $1,132 per share.
• The offer represents a 23.9% premium to unaffected closing prices on October 28.
• Marvell shareholders will own approximately 67% of the combined company, with Lumentum shareholders holding 33%.
The deal brings together Marvell's custom AI chips, networking switches and interconnect technology with Lumentum's lasers, optical transceivers and optical circuit switching capabilities.
The bigger opportunity is the growing shift from copper to optical connectivity as AI data centers require faster data transfer, greater bandwidth and improved power efficiency.
Marvell says the combination could expand its addressable market to $550B, allowing it to offer more integrated connectivity solutions across AI data centers and within advanced chip packages.
The companies also expect:
• More than $1B in annual run-rate cost synergies.
• Improved long-term revenue growth and profit margins.
• Accretion to Marvell's adjusted EPS in the first year after closing.
Matt Murphy will remain CEO of the combined company, with the transaction expected to close in H1 2028, subject to shareholder and regulatory approvals.
Separately, Marvell reaffirmed the midpoint of its fiscal Q3 revenue guidance while narrowing the expected range to ±2% from ±5%.
