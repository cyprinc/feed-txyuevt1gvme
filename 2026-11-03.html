Brookfield 计划在英国 🇬🇧 投资 270 亿美元建设 AI 数据中心
Brookfield 正与英国能源公司 Drax 及其 AI 基础设施平台 Radiant 合作，在英格兰北约克郡的 Drax 电站开发一座超大型数据中心。
该项目总投资（含 AI 芯片）可能超过 200 亿英镑（270 亿美元），并计划最终容量超过 1 吉瓦。
开发将分阶段进行：
• 第一阶段：到 2028 年初投运最多 100 兆瓦，需投资约 25 亿英镑。
• 第二阶段：在 2028 年至 2031 年间再增加 500 兆瓦。
• 第三阶段：在 2030 年代有望扩展到 1 吉瓦以上。
关键优势在于 Drax 现有的电力基础设施，包括电网接入、土地以及生物质发电能力。
数据中心初期将从电网取电，后续阶段计划直接接入电站，让 AI 基础设施使用现场发电。
初始融资将通过 Brookfield 与卡塔尔支持的 Qai 成立的合资企业提供，Radiant 将与 Drax 共同主导开发。
该项目仍处于早期阶段，仍需获得监管批准并找到主力租户。

原文：BROOKFIELD PLANS $27B AI DATA CENTER PROJECT IN THE 🇬🇧 UK
Brookfield is partnering with British energy company Drax and its AI infrastructure platform Radiant to develop a massive data center at Drax Power Station in North Yorkshire, England.
The project could involve more than £20B ($27B) in total investment, including AI chips, with plans to eventually exceed 1 GW of capacity.
The development would take place in phases:
• Phase 1: Up to 100 MW operational by early 2028, requiring approximately £2.5B in investment.
• Phase 2: An additional 500 MW targeted between 2028 and 2031.
• Phase 3: Potential expansion beyond 1 GW in the 2030s.
The key advantage is Drax's existing power infrastructure, including its grid connection, land and biomass power generation capacity.
Initially, the data center would draw electricity from the grid. Later phases aim to connect directly to the power station, allowing AI infrastructure to access on-site electricity generation.
Initial financing would come through Brookfield's joint venture with Qatar-backed Qai, while Radiant would lead development alongside Drax.
The project remains in its early stages and still requires regulatory approvals and an anchor tenant.

MIT计算机科学与人工智能实验室（CSAIL）的Rikiya Takehi及其同事展示了一项突破性技术：GPT-6 Astra能够将神经网络的权重作为文本读取，并对其进行精确修改，整个过程无需训练，也无需梯度计算。这种方法被称为“权重空间编程”（Weight-Space Programming）。

1. MATH-500数学测试：准确率从7.9%提升至48.1%。
2. GSM8K数学推理测试：准确率从13.8%提升至67.1%。
3. 指令遵循能力：评分提高至原来的3倍。
