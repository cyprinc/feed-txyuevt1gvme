Jukan (@jukan05)

回应：有人声称台积电将进入存储并颠覆三大存储厂
这说不通。据我所知，台积电这个项目已做了五年多，但若假定他们在做大宗 DRAM 就错了。台积电没有 DRAM IP、没有 DRAM 专利，也没有相关技术。
我认为“3D DRAM”这个词造成了误解。
与业内专家交流后，我认为正确理解台积电进入 DRAM 的报道应是：
台积电早前发表的研究能看出方向。
在 2025 VLSI Symposium 上，台积电展示了基于氧化物半导体的存储技术，单元为一晶体管一电容（1T1C）。目标是把这种存储集成进先进逻辑芯片，给处理器提供低功耗、低延迟缓存。
关键在于存储阵列直接建在逻辑芯片的后道互连层（BEOL）里。传统 DRAM 是单独造一颗芯片。
为了在先进逻辑芯片互连层里做存储阵列，台积电用了氧化物半导体沟道的选通晶体管，以及低温工艺做的电容。
据台积电，集成存储与逻辑的测试芯片工作在 0.75V。数据保持超过 128ms，随机读写周期低于 8ns，耐久性超过 10¹⁴ 次。台积电把它定位为比 SRAM 密度更高、同时保持低功耗与低延迟的处理器缓存。
所以台积电要做的 3D DRAM，是替代 SRAM 的下一代技术，而且不会是大批量。
谁说台积电会做 DDR 或 LPDDR，根本不懂在说什么。

原文：RE: The claim that TSMC will enter memory and disrupt the big three memory makers
This doesn't make sense. As far as I know, TSMC has been working on this project for more than five years, but it would be a mistake to assume what they're building is commodity DRAM. TSMC has no DRAM IP, no DRAM patents, and none of the relevant technology.
I think the term "3D DRAM" is what's causing the misunderstanding.
After talking with industry experts, I believe this is the correct way to read the reports of TSMC entering DRAM.
TSMC's earlier published research gives a good sense of the direction.
At the 2025 VLSI Symposium, TSMC presented a memory technology based on oxide semiconductors, using a cell made of one transistor and one capacitor (1T1C). The goal was to integrate this memory into advanced logic chips and give processors a cache with low power and low latency.
The key feature of the design is that the memory array is built directly into the back end of line (BEOL) interconnect layers of the logic chip. Conventional DRAM is manufactured as a separate chip.
To build the memory array in the interconnect layers of an advanced logic chip, TSMC used select transistors with an oxide semiconductor channel and capacitors fabricated with a low temperature process.
According to TSMC, the test chip, which integrates memory and logic on a single die, operated at 0.75V. Data retention exceeded 128ms, random read/write cycle time was under 8ns, and endurance was above 10¹⁴ cycles. TSMC positioned the technology as a processor cache that offers higher density than SRAM while keeping power consumption and latency low.
So TSMC would be producing 3D DRAM as a next generation technology to replace SRAM. And not in large volumes, either.
Anyone saying TSMC will make DDR or LPDDR has no idea what they're talking about.
